FacilPOS works with a range of standard point-of-sale hardware, including barcode scanners, receipt printers, cash drawers, and card payment terminals. Most devices connect through USB, Bluetooth, or your local network and require minimal configuration to get running. This guide covers the setup steps for each supported hardware type so your checkout station is ready for your first sale.

<Warning>
  Use only hardware that appears on the FacilPOS compatibility list for best results. Unlisted devices may work but are not officially supported, and FacilSoft cannot guarantee correct operation.
</Warning>

## Barcode Scanner

Barcode scanners let cashiers scan product barcodes instead of searching or typing product names, dramatically speeding up checkout.

**Compatible scanner types:**
- **USB HID** (Human Interface Device) — the most common type; recognised by the operating system as a keyboard
- **Bluetooth** — pairs with the POS device wirelessly; useful for counter-free setups

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect the scanner">
    Plug a USB HID scanner directly into a USB port on your POS device. No additional setup is required — the operating system recognises it automatically as a keyboard input device. For a Bluetooth scanner, pair it through your device's Bluetooth settings before proceeding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test the scanner in FacilPOS">
    Open a new sale in **POS → New Sale** and scan any product barcode. If the product appears in the cart, the scanner is working correctly. If nothing happens, click inside the search field first to ensure it has focus, then scan again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If your USB scanner is not recognised, check that it is configured for **HID keyboard emulation mode**. Refer to the scanner's printed manual for the programming barcode that enables this mode — this is a one-time setup done on the scanner itself.
</Tip>

## Receipt Printer

Receipt printers produce printed proof-of-purchase for customers and end-of-shift reports for your team.

**Supported connection types:** USB, Network (Ethernet or Wi-Fi), Bluetooth

Before configuring the printer in FacilPOS, make sure the printer is powered on, connected to your POS device or network, and that your operating system recognises it. Most modern receipt printers are detected automatically when connected via USB. For network printers, note the printer's IP address — you can usually find this by printing a self-test page (hold the feed button while powering on) or checking your router's connected device list.

### Configure the printer in FacilPOS

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Store Setup">
    Go to **Configuration → Store Setup** and scroll to the **Receipt Printer** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your printer">
    Choose your printer from the **Printer** dropdown. For network printers, enter the printer's IP address in the **Network Address** field — you can find this on the printer's self-test printout.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set paper width">
    Select **80 mm** from the **Paper Width** dropdown. This matches standard 80 mm thermal rolls used in most POS printers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and test">
    Click **Save**, then click **Test Print**. A test receipt should print immediately. If it does not, check that the printer is powered on and connected, then try again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Use **80 mm thermal paper** rolls for standard receipts. Thermal paper requires no ink and produces clean, fast printouts — avoid paper with a glossy or waxy coating, as it does not print well with thermal heads.
</Tip>

## Cash Drawer

Cash drawers open automatically on cash payments and can also be opened manually by authorised staff.

**Supported connection methods:**
- **Via receipt printer** — most cash drawers connect to the receipt printer using an RJ-11 (telephone-style) cable; the printer sends the drawer open signal when triggered
- **USB** — some drawers connect directly to the POS device via USB

### Configure auto-open on cash payment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Store Setup">
    Go to **Configuration → Store Setup** and find the **Cash Drawer** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable auto-open">
    Toggle **Auto-Open Drawer on Cash Payment** to on. If your drawer is connected via a receipt printer, select the correct printer from the **Connected Via** dropdown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. The drawer will now open automatically whenever a cash payment is completed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Open the drawer manually

Authorised users can open the cash drawer at any time from **POS → Dashboard** by clicking the **Open Cash Drawer** button. This action is logged in the session audit trail.

## Payment Terminal

Payment terminals handle card and contactless payments. FacilPOS communicates with the terminal over your local network.

Before pairing, make sure your terminal is powered on and connected to the same Wi-Fi or wired network as your POS device. If you are unsure how to connect your terminal to the network, refer to the quick-start guide that came with the terminal or contact your payment provider.

### Pair the terminal with FacilPOS

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the terminal's IP address">
    On the payment terminal, navigate to its network or settings menu and note the **IP address** displayed. If you cannot locate it, check your router's connected device list for the terminal's name or MAC address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Payment Methods configuration">
    In FacilPOS, go to **Configuration → Payment Methods → Card Terminal**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the IP address">
    Select **Network (IP)** as the connection type, then enter the terminal's IP address and port number as shown on the terminal's network settings screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pair the terminal">
    Click **Pair Terminal**. FacilPOS will send a connection request to the terminal. If the terminal displays a pairing prompt, confirm it on the terminal's screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run a test transaction">
    Click **Test Transaction** to verify the connection. Confirm the terminal responds with an approval message. Your terminal is now ready to process payments.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If the terminal stops responding after a network change (for example, after a router restart), go to **Configuration → Payment Methods → Card Terminal**, update the IP address if it has changed, and click **Pair Terminal** again to re-establish the connection.
</Note>

For payment terminal troubleshooting, see [Common Issues](/help/common-issues). For payment method configuration, see [Payment Methods](/configuration/payment-methods).
