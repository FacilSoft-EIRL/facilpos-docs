---
title: 'Troubleshooting Common FacilPOS Problems and Errors'
sidebarTitle: 'Common Issues'
description: 'Diagnose and resolve the most frequent FacilPOS problems, including login failures, payment errors, printer faults, stock sync issues, and more.'
---

Even in a well-configured system, day-to-day issues can arise — a cashier can't log in, a receipt printer goes silent, or stock levels look wrong after a busy shift. This page walks you through the most common FacilPOS problems and the quickest paths to resolving them. Work through the relevant steps before escalating to your system administrator.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Cannot log in / PIN not accepted">
    If your PIN is rejected at the login screen, try the following steps:

    1. Check that **Caps Lock** is off — PINs are numeric, but keyboard state can interfere with entry on some devices.
    2. Confirm you are entering the PIN assigned to your user account, not a previous one.
    3. Ask your FacilPOS administrator to reset your PIN via **Configuration → Users & Permissions → [Your User] → Reset PIN**.
    4. If the problem persists after a reset, contact your administrator to verify your account is active and has the correct role assigned.

    <Note>
      After five consecutive failed PIN attempts, your account may be temporarily locked. An administrator must unlock it from the Users & Permissions panel.
    </Note>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Sale not saving / session error">
    If a sale fails to save or you see a session-related error message, the most likely cause is that no register session is currently open.

    1. Navigate to **POS → Dashboard** and check whether an active session is displayed.
    2. If no session is open, click **Open Session** and enter your opening cash amount.
    3. Once a session is active, return to the sale and try again.
    4. If a session is already open but errors persist, refresh the page to re-establish the connection. Unsaved items in the cart will need to be re-added.

    <Warning>
      Do not close your browser tab mid-sale without first saving or voiding the transaction. Doing so may leave the session in an inconsistent state.
    </Warning>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Product not appearing in POS grid">
    Products missing from the sales grid are almost always caused by one of three configuration issues:

    1. **Product is inactive** — Go to **Inventory → Products**, find the product, and confirm its status is set to **Active**.
    2. **No category assigned** — The POS grid organises products by category. Ensure the product is assigned to at least one category in **Inventory → Categories**.
    3. **Stock is zero or stock tracking is blocking display** — If your POS is configured to hide out-of-stock items, either enable **Allow Sales Without Stock** for the product or replenish stock via **Inventory → Stock Management**.

    After making any of these changes, refresh the POS screen to reload the product grid.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Payment terminal not responding">
    If the card payment terminal is unresponsive during checkout, work through these steps in order:

    1. Verify the terminal is **powered on** and shows a ready or idle screen.
    2. Check the **network or serial cable** connection between the terminal and the POS device.
    3. Restart the terminal using its power button and wait for it to fully reboot.
    4. If the terminal still does not respond, re-pair it in FacilPOS: go to **Configuration → Payment Methods → Card Terminal** and follow the pairing instructions.
    5. For persistent issues, consult your terminal manufacturer's documentation or contact your payment provider.

    <Tip>
      Keep the terminal's IP address static (reserved in your router) to avoid re-pairing every time the network refreshes.
    </Tip>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Stock levels not updating after a sale">
    If inventory quantities in FacilERP do not reflect recent POS sales, the ERP sync may be delayed or disabled.

    1. Go to **Configuration → Integrations** and confirm that **ERP Sync** is toggled on.
    2. Check the **Last Sync** timestamp. If it is stale, click **Run Manual Sync** to trigger an immediate update.
    3. Review any sync error messages displayed on the Integrations page and resolve the flagged items.
    4. If sync errors reference specific products, verify those products exist and are correctly mapped in both FacilPOS and FacilERP.

    <Note>
      Sync frequency is controlled by your FacilERP configuration. Contact your administrator if you need real-time stock updates.
    </Note>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Receipt printer not working">
    A silent receipt printer is usually a connectivity or driver issue.

    1. Confirm the printer is **powered on** and shows no error lights or paper-jam indicators.
    2. Check the USB, network, or Bluetooth connection between the printer and the POS device.
    3. On Windows, open **Devices and Printers** and confirm the printer appears and is set as default. On macOS, check **System Settings → Printers & Scanners**.
    4. In FacilPOS, go to **Configuration → Store Setup → Receipt Printer** and click **Test Print** to send a test page.
    5. If the test print fails, re-select the printer in the Store Setup configuration and save.

    For full driver installation and connection instructions, see the [Hardware Setup guide](/help/hardware-setup).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Customer loyalty points not applied">
    Loyalty points are only awarded when two conditions are met before the payment is processed.

    1. Confirm the **loyalty program is enabled** in **Configuration → Store Setup → Loyalty Program**.
    2. Ensure the **customer is selected** on the sale before you tap or click the payment button — points cannot be applied retroactively after payment.
    3. Check that the customer's account is not suspended in **Customers → Customer Management**.
    4. Verify the product or category is not excluded from the loyalty program in the program settings.

    For a full explanation of how loyalty points are earned and redeemed, see [Loyalty & Discounts](/customers/loyalty-discounts).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Reports showing no data">
    Empty reports are almost always a filter or session issue rather than a data loss problem.

    1. Open the relevant report in **Reports** and check the **date range filter** — confirm it covers the period you expect to see data for.
    2. Sales data only appears in reports after a **register session is closed**. If a session from that period is still open, close it first.
    3. Verify you have the correct **store or register** selected if your report supports multi-location filtering.
    4. If you recently ran a manual ERP sync, allow a few minutes for report data to refresh.

    See [Sales Reports](/reports/sales-reports) and [Cash Register Reports](/reports/cash-register) for detailed filter guidance.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Refund failing">
    Failed refunds usually stem from a permissions issue or a missing original transaction.

    1. Confirm your user account has the **Supervisor** or **Manager** role — cashier-level accounts may not have refund permissions. Ask an administrator to grant access via **Configuration → Users & Permissions**.
    2. Search for the original transaction by receipt number or date in **POS → Returns & Refunds**. The original transaction must be found before a refund can be processed.
    3. For **card refunds**, the original payment terminal must be connected and paired — card refunds are processed back to the original card and require the terminal to be active.
    4. Cash refunds do not require the terminal but do require sufficient cash in the drawer.

    For step-by-step refund instructions, see [Returns & Refunds](/pos/returns-refunds).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Price list not applying at checkout">
    If the expected price list is not being applied to a sale, check the following:

    1. Confirm the price list is **active** in **Inventory → Price Lists** — inactive lists are ignored at checkout.
    2. Verify the **customer is assigned** to the correct price list in **Customers → Customer Management → [Customer] → Price List**.
    3. Ensure the **customer is selected** on the current sale before adding products — price lists are applied when the customer is attached, not after items are already in the cart.
    4. Check that the products in the sale are **included** in the price list; some lists are scoped to specific categories or items.

    For more on setting up and managing price lists, see [Price Lists](/inventory/price-lists).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
