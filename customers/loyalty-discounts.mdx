FacilPOS gives you a flexible toolkit to reward customers and incentivize purchases — from simple percentage discounts applied at the register to a fully automated loyalty point program that tracks balances across every visit. All discount and loyalty settings are configured once in the back office and then apply automatically at checkout, reducing manual effort for your staff and ensuring consistent treatment for every customer.

---

## Discounts

### Types of Discounts

FacilPOS supports three discount types, each suited to a different promotion strategy:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Percentage Discount" icon="percent" href="/pos/new-sale">
    Reduces the price by a percentage (e.g., 15% off). Works at the item level or the entire order.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Fixed Amount Discount" icon="dollar-sign" href="/pos/new-sale">
    Subtracts a fixed currency value (e.g., $5 off). Useful for vouchers and gift card promotions.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Buy X Get Y" icon="gift" href="/pos/new-sale">
    Automatically adds a free or discounted item when a qualifying quantity is reached (e.g., buy 2, get 1 free).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Price Lists" icon="list" href="/inventory/price-lists">
    Assign customer-specific or group-specific prices that override the standard catalog price automatically.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

### Item-Level vs. Order-Level Discounts

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Item-Level Discount">
    An item-level discount applies to a single line in the current sale. Use this when you want to reduce the price of one specific product without affecting the rest of the order.

    - The discounted price appears on the receipt line for that item.
    - You can apply different discount types to different items in the same transaction.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Order-Level Discount">
    An order-level discount applies to the entire transaction total after all item prices are summed. Use this for blanket promotions, negotiated deals, or loyalty redemptions.

    - The discount appears as a separate line on the receipt.
    - Order-level discounts are calculated after item-level discounts, so both can co-exist.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### Applying a Discount During a Sale

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the active sale">
    Navigate to the [New Sale](/pos/new-sale) screen with items already added to the order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the discount target">
    To discount a specific item, tap the item line and select **Discount**. To discount the whole order, tap the **Order Discount** button at the bottom of the order panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the discount type and value">
    Select **Percentage**, **Fixed Amount**, or **Buy X Get Y**, then enter the value. The order total updates in real time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the discount">
    Tap **Apply**. If the discount exceeds the configured approval threshold (see below), FacilPOS will prompt for supervisor authorization before proceeding.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Supervisor Approval for High-Value Discounts

You can protect margins by requiring manager authorization for discounts above a set threshold.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open discount settings">
    Go to **Configuration → Store Setup → Discount Policy**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the approval threshold">
    Enter the maximum discount percentage or fixed amount a standard cashier can apply without approval. Any discount above this value triggers a supervisor prompt at the register.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize at the register">
    When prompted, the supervisor enters their PIN or swipes their badge to approve the discount. The approval is logged with the supervisor's name for audit purposes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Discount thresholds are configured per user role. Ensure your cashier and supervisor roles are correctly set up in [Users & Permissions](/configuration/users-permissions) before enabling this feature.
</Warning>

## Loyalty Points

### Enabling the Loyalty Program

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to loyalty settings">
    Go to **Configuration → Store Setup → Loyalty Program** and toggle **Enable Loyalty Program** to **On**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the earning rate">
    Define how many points a customer earns per currency unit spent — for example, **1 point per $1**. You can set different rates for specific product categories or customer groups.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the redemption value">
    Define how much each point is worth when redeemed — for example, **100 points = $1 discount**. FacilPOS converts the customer's available balance to a discount value automatically at checkout.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Points Earning Rules

<Accordion title="Standard earning rule">
  By default, customers earn points on every sale based on the rate you configure. Points are credited to the customer's account when the [payment](/pos/payments) is completed — not when the sale is opened.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Category-specific multipliers">
  You can assign a points multiplier to specific product categories. For example, set 2× points on beverages during a promotional week. Navigate to **Inventory → Categories**, open a category, and set the **Loyalty Multiplier** field.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Minimum purchase threshold">
  Optionally require a minimum order total before points are awarded. Configure this under **Configuration → Store Setup → Loyalty Program → Minimum Earning Amount**.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Excluded items">
  Certain items — such as gift cards, deposits, or already-discounted products — can be excluded from point earning. Open the product in [Products](/inventory/products) and toggle **Exclude from Loyalty**.
</Accordion>

### Redeeming Points at Checkout

<Steps>
  <Step title="Attach the customer to the sale">
    Search for and select the customer on the [New Sale](/pos/new-sale) screen. Their current point balance appears next to their name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Redeem Points">
    In the order panel, click **Redeem Points**. FacilPOS shows the maximum redeemable amount based on the customer's balance and any redemption cap you have configured.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the redemption">
    Enter the number of points to redeem or accept the maximum. The equivalent discount is applied to the order total, and the points are deducted from the customer's balance upon payment completion.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Redeemed points are deducted only after the transaction is fully paid. If a sale is voided, the points are automatically reinstated to the customer's account.
</Note>

### Viewing a Customer's Point Balance

Open the customer's profile from **Customers → Customer List** and select the **Loyalty** tab. You will see:

<ResponseField name="Current Balance" type="integer">
  Total unredeemed points available for the customer.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="Lifetime Points Earned" type="integer">
  Cumulative points earned across all transactions since enrollment.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="Lifetime Points Redeemed" type="integer">
  Total points the customer has redeemed as discounts.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="Last Activity" type="date">
  Date of the most recent points-earning or redemption event.
</ResponseField>

## Promotional Offers

### Creating a Time-Limited Promotion

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Promotions manager">
    Navigate to **Configuration → Promotions → New Promotion**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Define the promotion details">
    <ParamField body="Promotion Name" type="string" required>
      A clear, descriptive label for this promotion (e.g., *Weekend Drinks 20% Off*).
    </ParamField>
    <ParamField body="Discount Type" type="select" required>
      Choose Percentage, Fixed Amount, or Buy X Get Y.
    </ParamField>
    <ParamField body="Applies To" type="select" required>
      Scope the promotion to a specific product, category, or the entire order.
    </ParamField>
    <ParamField body="Start Date / End Date" type="datetime" required>
      The promotion activates and deactivates automatically at the times you set — no manual intervention needed.
    </ParamField>
    <ParamField body="Customer Group" type="select">
      Restrict the promotion to a specific customer group (e.g., VIP only). Leave blank to apply to all customers.
    </ParamField>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and activate">
    Click **Save**. The promotion becomes active at the configured start date and expires automatically at the end date.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Auto-Apply vs. Manual Coupon Code

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Auto-Apply">
    Set the promotion to **Auto-Apply** and FacilPOS will detect qualifying items or order conditions automatically during a sale. No action is required from the cashier.

    - Best for store-wide sales, category promotions, and Buy X Get Y offers.
    - Multiple auto-apply promotions can stack if you allow stacking in **Configuration → Promotions → Stacking Rules**.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Manual Coupon Code">
    Assign a unique coupon code to the promotion. The cashier (or customer, in self-checkout mode) enters the code during the sale to activate the discount.

    1. In the Promotions manager, toggle **Require Coupon Code** to **On**.
    2. Enter a code (e.g., `DRINKS20`) or click **Generate Random Code**.
    3. Distribute the code via email, printed flyer, or your loyalty communications.

    <Info>
      You can limit coupon codes to a single use per customer or set a maximum total redemption count under **Advanced Options** in the Promotions manager.
    </Info>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Tip>
  Combine a customer group [price list](/inventory/price-lists) with a loyalty program for a fully automated VIP tier. Assign wholesale or VIP customers to a discounted price list and a higher points multiplier so their benefits apply at checkout without any manual steps.
</Tip>
