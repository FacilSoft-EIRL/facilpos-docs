Customer accounts in FacilPOS let you build lasting relationships by attaching a name, contact details, and purchase history to every transaction. Whether you run a retail shop, a wholesale operation, or a multi-tier VIP program, centralizing customer data in FacilPOS means your staff always has the right context at the point of sale — and every record is kept up to date across your FacilERP back office automatically.

## Creating a Customer

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Customers panel">
    From the main menu, navigate to **Customers → Customer List**, then click **New Customer**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter basic information">
    Fill in the required fields:

    <ParamField body="Full Name" type="string" required>
      The customer's full name or company name as it will appear on receipts and invoices.
    </ParamField>
    <ParamField body="Phone" type="string">
      Primary contact number used for quick lookup at checkout.
    </ParamField>
    <ParamField body="Email" type="string">
      Used for digital receipts and promotional communications.
    </ParamField>
    <ParamField body="Tax ID / VAT Number" type="string">
      Required when issuing tax invoices. FacilPOS prints this number automatically on the invoice.
    </ParamField>
    <ParamField body="Assigned Price List" type="select">
      Link a specific [price list](/inventory/price-lists) to this customer so their prices apply automatically at checkout.
    </ParamField>
    <ParamField body="Notes" type="string">
      Internal comments visible to staff — for example, preferred payment method or delivery instructions.
    </ParamField>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the profile">
    Click **Save**. The customer record is created immediately and available across your FacilERP back office.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Always link a customer to a sale when issuing tax invoices. FacilPOS pulls the customer's Tax ID directly into the invoice, ensuring compliance with local tax regulations without manual entry.
</Tip>

## Searching for a Customer at Checkout

You can find a customer from the active sale screen without leaving the transaction.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the customer search">
    On the [New Sale](/pos/new-sale) screen, click the **Customer** field at the top of the order panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type your search term">
    Search by **name**, **phone number**, or **customer ID**. Results appear instantly as you type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the customer">
    Click the matching result to attach the customer to the current sale. Their assigned price list and any active loyalty points load automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If no match is found, you can create a new customer on the spot by clicking **+ New Customer** in the search dropdown without abandoning the current sale.
</Note>

## Viewing Purchase History

A customer's complete purchase history is available directly from their profile.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the customer profile">
    Go to **Customers → Customer List** and click on the customer's name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the Purchase History tab">
    Switch to the **Purchase History** tab to see a chronological list of all transactions, including date, items purchased, total amount, and payment method.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Drill into a transaction">
    Click any transaction row to open the original receipt or invoice. From there you can reprint, email, or initiate a [return or refund](/pos/returns-refunds).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Editing and Deactivating Customers

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Edit a Customer">
    1. Open the customer's profile from **Customers → Customer List**.
    2. Click **Edit** in the top-right corner.
    3. Update any field — contact details, assigned price list, Tax ID, or notes.
    4. Click **Save Changes**. Updates are reflected across your FacilERP back office automatically.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Deactivate a Customer">
    1. Open the customer's profile.
    2. Click **Edit**, then toggle **Status** to **Inactive**.
    3. Click **Save Changes**.

    Inactive customers no longer appear in checkout search results but their purchase history is fully preserved for reporting and auditing purposes.

    <Warning>
      Deactivating a customer does not delete their data or affect past transactions. To permanently remove a record, contact your system administrator.
    </Warning>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Customer Groups

Customer groups let you organize accounts by type — for example, **Retail**, **Wholesale**, or **VIP** — and apply shared settings like price lists, discount policies, and loyalty tiers to the entire group at once.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a group">
    Navigate to **Customers → Customer Groups** and click **New Group**. Give it a descriptive name (e.g., *Wholesale Partners*) and optionally assign a default price list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign customers to the group">
    Open any customer profile, click **Edit**, and choose the appropriate group from the **Customer Group** dropdown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply group-level settings">
    Settings configured at the group level — such as a linked [price list](/inventory/price-lists) or discount threshold — override individual defaults unless explicitly overridden on the customer record itself.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Customer groups also drive segmentation in [Sales Reports](/reports/sales-reports), letting you compare revenue, average order value, and visit frequency across retail, wholesale, and VIP segments.
</Info>
