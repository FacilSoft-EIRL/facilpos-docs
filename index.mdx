## Getting Started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set Up Your Store">
    Log in to FacilERP and navigate to **FacilPOS → Configuration → Store Setup**. Enter your business name, tax settings, and currency.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Your Products">
    Go to **Inventory → Products** to create your product catalog. Assign categories, set prices, and configure stock levels.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Payment Methods">
    Under **Configuration → Payment Methods**, enable cash, card, or any other payment types your store accepts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a Register Session">
    Head to **Point of Sale → Dashboard**, open a new cash register session, and start processing sales.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  FacilPOS is fully integrated with FacilERP — changes to products, pricing, and customers sync automatically across all modules.
</Tip>
