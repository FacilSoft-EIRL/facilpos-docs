Sales reports in FacilPOS give you a real-time and historical view of your business performance — from total daily revenue down to which cashier closed the most transactions. Every sale processed at the point of sale is captured automatically, so your reports are always up to date without any manual data entry.

## Navigate to Sales Reports

To access your sales reports, go to **FacilPOS → Reports → Sales**. From there, select the report type you want to view from the left-hand panel.

## Filter by Date Range

Every report supports flexible date filtering so you can view data for any period you need.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the report">
    Select a report type from **FacilPOS → Reports → Sales**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the date range">
    Click the **Date Range** picker at the top of the report. Choose a preset — **Today**, **This Week**, **This Month**, **Last 30 Days** — or set a custom start and end date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply the filter">
    Click **Apply** to reload the report with data for your selected period.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Export Reports

You can export any sales report to share with stakeholders or import into external tools.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Export to CSV">
    With your report open and date range applied, click **Export → CSV**. The file downloads immediately and contains all visible rows, ready for use in spreadsheet applications.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Export to PDF">
    Click **Export → PDF** to generate a formatted, print-ready document. The PDF includes your store name, selected date range, and a summary totals row at the bottom.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## How Sales Data Flows to FacilERP

FacilPOS is fully integrated with the FacilERP accounting module. Every completed sale automatically generates a corresponding accounting entry — no manual sync required.

<Info>
  Sales totals, tax collected, and payment method breakdowns are posted to your FacilERP chart of accounts in real time. Your accountant can review these entries directly in FacilERP without waiting for an end-of-day export.
</Info>

To review how transactions appear on the accounting side, open **FacilERP → Accounting → Journal Entries** and filter by the **POS** source.

<Tip>
  Run the **Sales by Cashier** report at the end of each shift to monitor individual team performance. Tracking average ticket value alongside transaction count helps you identify coaching opportunities and reward top performers.
</Tip>
