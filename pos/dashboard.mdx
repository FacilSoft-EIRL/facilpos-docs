The FacilPOS dashboard is your central workspace for every sales interaction. From here you can open a register session, search for products, build a sale, select a customer, and track your shift totals — all without leaving a single screen. Getting familiar with the dashboard layout before your first transaction will save you time throughout the day.

## Opening FacilPOS from FacilERP

To reach the POS module, log in to FacilERP and select **FacilPOS** from the module switcher in the top navigation bar. The dashboard loads automatically and displays the status of your current register session. If no session is active, FacilPOS prompts you to open one before you can process any sales.

## Opening a Cash Register Session

Every shift begins with an open register session that records your starting balance and ties all transactions to a specific operator and terminal.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click 'Open Session'">
    On the dashboard welcome screen, click the **Open Session** button. If you are already logged in from a previous session, FacilPOS will ask you to confirm that the previous session was properly closed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your opening balance">
    Count the cash in your drawer and type the total amount into the **Opening Balance** field. This figure becomes the baseline for your end-of-shift cash reconciliation report.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm and start">
    Click **Start Session**. The dashboard activates and displays your session start time and operator name in the status bar at the top of the screen.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Always open a register session before processing your first sale. FacilPOS blocks payment processing until an active session exists, so starting this step first prevents interruptions during busy periods.
</Tip>

## Dashboard Sections

Once your session is open, the dashboard is divided into several functional areas that work together during a sale.

<Accordion title="Search Bar">
  The search bar sits at the top of the product panel. Type a product name, SKU, or scan a barcode to instantly locate items. Results filter in real time as you type, so you rarely need to press Enter.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Product Grid">
  Below the search bar, the product grid displays your most frequently sold items as tiles for quick tap-or-click access. You can configure which products appear here in [Store Setup](/configuration/store-setup).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Current Sale Cart">
  The right-hand panel shows the active sale in progress — line items, quantities, individual prices, and the running subtotal. Every product you add from the grid or search bar appears here immediately.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Customer Selector">
  Above the cart total, the customer selector lets you attach a customer record to the current sale. Click **Add Customer**, then search by name, phone number, or email. Attaching a customer enables loyalty tracking and pre-fills receipt delivery details.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Payment Button">
  At the bottom of the cart panel, the **Charge** button displays the current order total and opens the payment screen when clicked. The button remains active only while your register session is open and at least one item is in the cart.
</Accordion>

## Session Summary and Shift Totals

The dashboard status bar shows a live count of transactions processed and the cumulative sales total for your current session. This gives you an at-a-glance view of shift performance without running a full report. For a detailed breakdown, close your session and review the [Cash Register Report](/reports/cash-register).
