FacilPOS supports multiple payment methods so you can serve customers however they prefer to pay. From the payment screen you can accept cash, process a card through a connected terminal, divide a total across more than one method, or apply store credit and gift cards — all within the same checkout flow. Once payment is confirmed, FacilPOS logs the transaction, updates your session totals, and triggers receipt generation.

## Selecting a Payment Method

After clicking **Charge** on the sale screen, the payment screen displays the order total and a set of payment method buttons. Select the method that matches how the customer is paying, then follow the steps for that method below.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Cash">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Select Cash">
        Click **Cash** on the payment screen. A numeric keypad appears alongside the **Amount Tendered** field.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Enter the amount tendered">
        Type the amount the customer hands you, or use the quick-denomination buttons (e.g., $5, $10, $20) for common bills. FacilPOS instantly calculates and displays the **Change Due** beneath the tendered field.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Confirm the payment">
        Click **Confirm Payment**. The transaction is recorded, and the change amount remains on screen until you dismiss it so you can count back the correct change to the customer.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Info>
      If the customer pays the exact amount, FacilPOS displays "No Change Due" and completes the transaction in one click.
    </Info>
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Card">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Select Card">
        Click **Card** on the payment screen. FacilPOS sends the payment request to your configured payment terminal.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Customer completes payment on the terminal">
        Ask the customer to swipe, insert, or tap their card on the terminal. The terminal handles chip, magnetic stripe, and contactless (NFC) transactions, including mobile wallets like Apple Pay and Google Pay.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Wait for terminal confirmation">
        FacilPOS waits for the terminal to return an approval or decline response. An **Approved** status confirms the payment; a **Declined** status returns you to the payment screen to try another method.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Warning>
      Card payments require a payment terminal that has been configured in FacilPOS. If the terminal is not responding, verify the connection in [Payment Methods](/configuration/payment-methods) before retrying.
    </Warning>
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Split Payment">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Select Split Payment">
        Click **Split Payment** on the payment screen. A breakdown panel appears showing the full order total and fields for each payment method you want to use.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Enter the amount for the first method">
        Choose the first payment method (e.g., Cash) and enter the partial amount the customer is paying with it.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Add the remaining method(s)">
        Click **Add Payment Method** and select the next method (e.g., Card). FacilPOS pre-fills the remaining balance automatically. Adjust if needed.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Confirm all payments">
        Complete each payment method in sequence — cash tender is confirmed immediately, while card methods wait for terminal approval. Once all methods are settled and the total reaches zero, FacilPOS records the full transaction.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Applying Gift Cards and Store Credit

If your store issues gift cards or store credit, you can apply them at the payment screen as a partial or full payment.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Gift Card or Store Credit">
    On the payment screen, click **Gift Card** or **Store Credit** depending on what the customer has.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the code or look up the customer">
    For gift cards, scan the barcode or type the card number manually. For store credit, attach a customer record to the sale (if not already done) and FacilPOS displays the available credit balance automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply and settle the remainder">
    FacilPOS deducts the gift card or credit value from the order total. If a balance remains, select a secondary payment method to cover it using the split payment flow.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Payment Confirmation

After a successful payment, FacilPOS displays a confirmation screen with the transaction ID, payment method(s) used, and the amount charged. This confirmation triggers:

- The **receipt prompt**, where you choose to print, email, or skip — see [Receipts](/pos/receipts).
- An automatic update to your **session totals** on the dashboard.
- An **ERP sync** that posts the transaction to FacilERP's accounting module.

To configure available payment methods or connect a new terminal, visit [Payment Methods](/configuration/payment-methods).
