Access control in FacilPOS ensures that every team member can do exactly what their role requires — and nothing more. By assigning roles to each user, you protect sensitive operations like refunds and configuration changes while keeping the checkout experience fast and simple for frontline staff.

## Built-in Roles

FacilPOS ships with three built-in roles. Each role is additive — higher roles include all permissions from the roles below them.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Cashier" icon="cash-register" href="/configuration/users-permissions">
    The default role for frontline staff. Cashiers can:
    - Open and close their own session
    - Process sales and accept payments
    - Apply standard discounts
    - Issue receipts (print and email)
    - Look up product prices and stock levels
  </Card>
  <Card title="Supervisor" icon="user-check" href="/configuration/users-permissions">
    Designed for team leads and shift supervisors. Supervisors can do everything a Cashier can, plus:
    - Apply large or custom discounts (above the cashier threshold)
    - Process returns and refunds
    - View daily sales and cash register reports
    - Override a declined payment
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Card title="Manager / Admin" icon="shield-halved" href="/configuration/users-permissions">
  Full access to FacilPOS. Managers can do everything a Supervisor can, plus:
  - Access all configuration settings (store setup, payment methods, integrations)
  - Create, edit, and deactivate user accounts
  - View all reports across all registers and date ranges
  - Manage products, categories, and price lists
  - Trigger and review ERP sync operations
</Card>

## Creating a New User

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to User Management">
    Go to **FacilPOS → Configuration → Users & Permissions** and click **Add User**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Personal Details">
    Fill in the following fields:

    <ParamField body="Full Name" type="string" required>
      The user's display name, shown in the audit log and on receipts if the cashier name field is enabled.
    </ParamField>

    <ParamField body="Username" type="string" required>
      A unique login handle. Lowercase letters, numbers, and underscores only. Cannot be changed after creation.
    </ParamField>

    <ParamField body="Email Address" type="string" required>
      Used for password reset and FacilERP notifications. Must be unique across the system.
    </ParamField>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign a Role">
    Select the appropriate role from the **Role** dropdown: **Cashier**, **Supervisor**, or **Manager/Admin**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a PIN">
    Enter a 4–6 digit PIN in the **Quick-Login PIN** field. The PIN is used for fast cashier switching at the register without requiring a full password entry.

    <Note>
      PINs must be unique across all active users. FacilPOS will alert you if the PIN is already taken.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the User">
    Click **Create User**. The new account is immediately active. The user will receive an email invitation to set their full password.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Editing and Deactivating Users

To update a user's name, email, role, or PIN, click the user's row in the user list and select **Edit**. All changes take effect immediately.

To remove access for a departing team member, click **Deactivate** rather than deleting the account. Deactivated users cannot log in, but their transaction history and audit log entries are preserved for reporting purposes.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Edit a User">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Find the User">
        Go to **Configuration → Users & Permissions** and use the search bar to locate the user.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Open the Edit Panel">
        Click the user's row and select **Edit User**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Make Changes">
        Update the relevant fields. You can change the role, email, display name, and PIN. The username cannot be changed.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Save">
        Click **Save Changes**. The updated permissions apply on the user's next action.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Deactivate a User">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Find the User">
        Locate the user in **Configuration → Users & Permissions**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Deactivate">
        Click the user's row, select **Deactivate**, and confirm the prompt. The user's status changes to **Inactive** and they are immediately signed out of any active session.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Reactivate if Needed">
        To restore access, find the user in the **Inactive** tab, click their row, and select **Reactivate**.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## PIN Login for Cashiers

PIN login allows cashiers to switch quickly between operators at a shared register without signing out and back in through the full login screen.

When a cashier finishes their turn at the register, they tap **Switch Cashier** on the POS screen. The next cashier enters their PIN and the session transfers to their account instantly. All subsequent sales are recorded under the new cashier's name.

<Tip>
  Encourage cashiers to switch rather than walk away from an open session. This keeps your audit log accurate and prevents one cashier's sales from being attributed to another.
</Tip>

## Audit Log

Every action taken in FacilPOS — sales, refunds, discounts, configuration changes, logins — is recorded in the audit log with the user's name, timestamp, and a description of the action.

To review the audit log, go to **Configuration → Users & Permissions → Audit Log**. Use the date range and user filters to narrow results. The log is read-only and cannot be edited or deleted.

<Info>
  The audit log retains records for 12 months by default. Contact your FacilERP administrator to adjust the retention period.
</Info>

## Sync with FacilERP

<Note>
  User accounts in FacilPOS are linked directly to FacilERP user accounts. Creating or editing a user in FacilPOS updates the corresponding FacilERP record, and vice versa. You do not need to manage users in two places.
</Note>

<Warning>
  Only users with the **Manager/Admin** role can create, edit, or deactivate user accounts. Supervisors and Cashiers do not have access to the Users & Permissions section.
</Warning>
