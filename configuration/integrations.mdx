FacilPOS is built as a native module of FacilERP, which means your point-of-sale data does not live in isolation. Every sale, refund, stock movement, and customer interaction can automatically flow into the accounting ledger, purchasing workflow, CRM, and HR systems — eliminating manual data entry and keeping every department working from the same source of truth.

<Note>
  All integrations described on this page require the corresponding FacilERP module to be active in your subscription. If a module is unavailable, its integration section will be greyed out in the configuration panel. Contact your FacilERP account manager to activate additional modules.
</Note>

## Accounting Integration

Every sale and refund processed in FacilPOS creates a journal entry in FacilERP Accounting automatically. You do not need to import or export files — the data flows in real time.

### What Gets Posted

| POS Event | Accounting Entry |
|---|---|
| Sale completed | Debit: Payment account; Credit: Revenue account |
| Tax collected | Credit: Tax liability account |
| Refund issued | Reversal of the original sale entry |
| Store credit issued | Debit: Revenue; Credit: Store credit liability |
| Cash discount applied | Debit: Discount expense; Credit: Revenue (net amount) |

### Configuring Accounting Accounts

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Accounting Integration Settings">
    Go to **FacilPOS → Configuration → Integrations → Accounting**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Map Revenue Accounts">
    Assign a FacilERP **Revenue Account** to each product category. Sales in that category will credit the mapped account. If a category has no mapping, the **Default Revenue Account** is used.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Map Tax Accounts">
    Assign a **Tax Liability Account** for each tax rate you have configured. This is the account that accumulates the tax you owe to the authorities.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Map Payment Accounts">
    For each payment method (cash, card, store credit), assign the corresponding asset or liability account in your chart of accounts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and Verify">
    Click **Save**. Process a test transaction and verify the journal entry in **FacilERP → Accounting → Journal Entries**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Purchasing / Stock Integration

FacilPOS and FacilERP Purchasing share a single inventory ledger. Stock levels visible on the POS screen are the same records that purchasing managers use to create and receive purchase orders.

### How It Works

- **Stock deductions**: Each sale immediately reduces the stock quantity in FacilERP. Returns add it back.
- **Purchase order receipts**: When a purchase order is received in FacilERP, the new stock is instantly available at the POS register.
- **Low-stock alerts**: Set a reorder point on any product in **Inventory → Products**. When stock falls below that point, FacilPOS triggers an alert and can optionally create a draft purchase order in FacilERP Purchasing for a manager to review and send.

### Configuring Low-Stock Automation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set Reorder Points">
    In **Inventory → Products**, open a product and set the **Reorder Point** (minimum quantity before an alert fires) and the **Reorder Quantity** (how many units to order).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable Draft PO Creation">
    In **Configuration → Integrations → Purchasing**, toggle on **Auto-create draft purchase orders on low stock**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign a Default Supplier">
    Select the default supplier for each product (or product category) so that draft purchase orders are addressed correctly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Draft purchase orders are never sent automatically — a manager must review and confirm them in FacilERP Purchasing before they are dispatched to a supplier.
</Tip>

## CRM / Customer Integration

Customer records are shared across FacilPOS and FacilERP CRM. A customer created at the POS register is immediately visible in CRM, and contact updates made in CRM appear at the register in real time.

### What Syncs

| Data | Direction | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Customer name, email, phone | Bidirectional | Updates in either system sync to the other |
| Purchase history | POS → CRM | Full transaction history accessible from the CRM contact card |
| Loyalty points balance | Bidirectional | Points earned at POS are redeemable at POS; CRM can adjust balances manually |
| Customer segments / tags | CRM → POS | Segments created in CRM can trigger automatic discounts at checkout |

### Configuring CRM Sync

Go to **Configuration → Integrations → CRM / Customers** and confirm that the **Customer Sync** toggle is enabled. No further mapping is required — the integration uses a shared customer ID that exists across both modules.

## HR / Payroll Integration

Cashier sessions in FacilPOS can feed directly into FacilERP HR attendance records, giving your HR team visibility into shift hours without requiring staff to clock in separately.

### How It Works

When a cashier opens a session, a clock-in event is sent to FacilERP HR. When the session closes, a clock-out event is recorded. The duration appears in the employee's attendance log alongside any other clock-in methods your business uses.

### Configuring HR Sync

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable Attendance Sync">
    Go to **Configuration → Integrations → HR / Payroll** and toggle on **Sync cashier sessions to HR attendance**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Match POS Users to HR Employees">
    FacilPOS attempts to match users automatically by their shared FacilERP user account. Review the mapping table and manually link any users that were not matched automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Session Events to Sync">
    Select whether to sync **session open**, **session close**, or both. Most businesses sync both to capture full shift durations.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Break times are not tracked automatically by FacilPOS. If your HR policy requires break logging, staff should use FacilERP HR's dedicated break tracking feature separately.
</Info>

## Data Sync Frequency

By default, all integrations operate in **real-time** mode — data flows to connected modules as events occur. If your network or server load requires it, you can switch to **end-of-day batch** mode.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Real-Time (Default)">
    - Journal entries post within seconds of a completed sale
    - Stock levels update instantly at all registers
    - Customer loyalty balances reflect immediately after each transaction

    Real-time sync is recommended for most businesses and is the default for all new installations.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="End-of-Day Batch">
    - All transactions from the day are bundled and posted at a scheduled time (configurable, e.g., 11:00 PM)
    - Stock levels at other registers may be slightly delayed during the trading day
    - Reduces integration load on busy networks

    To switch to batch mode, go to **Configuration → Integrations → Sync Settings**, select **End-of-Day Batch**, and set the batch processing time.

    <Warning>
      In batch mode, stock levels displayed across registers may not reflect sales made during the day. This can lead to overselling on low-stock items. Use real-time mode if stock accuracy across registers is critical.
    </Warning>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Troubleshooting Sync Issues

If data is not flowing between FacilPOS and other FacilERP modules, work through the following checks:

<Accordion title="Journal entries are not appearing in Accounting">
  1. Confirm the Accounting integration is enabled in **Configuration → Integrations → Accounting**.
  2. Verify that all payment methods have an assigned accounting account — unlinked payment types will cause the entry to fail silently.
  3. Check **FacilERP → Accounting → Sync Log** for error messages. Common causes include a missing account mapping or a locked accounting period.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Stock levels are not updating at other registers">
  1. Check the **Sync Mode** — if you are in batch mode, levels will not update until the batch runs.
  2. Go to **Configuration → Integrations → Purchasing** and click **Force Sync Now** to manually trigger an immediate stock update.
  3. If the issue persists, check the integration log under **Configuration → Integrations → Sync Log** for connectivity errors.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Customer data is out of sync between POS and CRM">
  1. Confirm the CRM integration toggle is enabled.
  2. Open the customer record in both FacilPOS and FacilERP CRM and note which fields differ.
  3. Click **Sync Now** on the customer record in FacilERP CRM to force a reconciliation. If conflicts exist, the CRM record takes precedence.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Cashier sessions are not appearing in HR attendance">
  1. Confirm the HR sync toggle is enabled and the affected cashier is mapped to an HR employee record.
  2. Check that the cashier's FacilERP user account is linked to an HR employee profile in **FacilERP → HR → Employees**.
  3. Review the **Integrations → Sync Log** for any permission errors — the HR module requires the POS integration user to have write access to attendance records.
</Accordion>
