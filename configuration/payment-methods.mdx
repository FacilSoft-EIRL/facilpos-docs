Payment methods control what tender options appear to your cashiers at checkout. FacilPOS supports cash, integrated card terminals, store credit, bank transfers, and fully custom payment types — each with its own configuration options. Enabling only the methods your business actively uses keeps the checkout screen clean and reduces cashier errors.

## Navigating to Payment Settings

Go to **FacilPOS → Configuration → Payment Methods**. You need a **Manager/Admin** role to make changes here. Each payment method listed has an **Enabled** toggle and a settings panel.

---

## Available Payment Types

<Accordion title="Cash">
  Cash is always available in FacilPOS and cannot be fully disabled. You can, however, control how change is calculated and rounded.

  <ParamField body="Rounding Rule" type="select">
    Choose how cash totals are rounded at the register:
    - **No rounding** — exact amounts only
    - **Round to nearest 0.05** — for currencies that have discontinued small coins
    - **Round to nearest 0.10** or **1.00** — for simplified cash handling

    Rounding adjustments appear as a separate line on the receipt so the transaction remains transparent.
  </ParamField>

  <ParamField body="Prompt for Amount Tendered" type="boolean">
    When enabled, the cashier must enter the amount of cash received from the customer. FacilPOS then calculates and displays the exact change due.
  </ParamField>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Credit / Debit Card">
  Card payments require a physical payment terminal paired with FacilPOS. Once paired, card transactions are initiated from the POS screen and confirmed on the terminal — the cashier never manually enters card details.

  See [Card Terminal Integration](#card-terminal-integration) below for pairing instructions.

  <ParamField body="Accepted Card Networks" type="multiselect">
    Select which networks your terminal accepts (e.g., Visa, Mastercard, Amex). This controls which icons appear on the payment screen — your terminal provider governs the actual acceptance rules.
  </ParamField>

  <ParamField body="Prompt for Tip" type="boolean">
    When enabled, the terminal prompts the customer to add a tip before authorizing the payment. Tip amounts are recorded separately in sales reports.
  </ParamField>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Store Credit">
  Store credit is issued automatically when you process a refund without returning to the original payment method. Customers can redeem their balance at checkout.

  <ParamField body="Allow Partial Redemption" type="boolean">
    When enabled, customers can pay part of the total with store credit and cover the remainder with another payment method.
  </ParamField>

  <ParamField body="Expiry Period" type="number">
    Number of days after which unused store credit expires. Set to `0` for no expiry.
  </ParamField>

  <Note>
    Store credit balances are linked to customer accounts. A customer must be selected at the start of the sale for this payment option to appear at checkout.
  </Note>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Bank Transfer">
  Bank transfer is intended for B2B and wholesale orders where the customer pays by wire transfer after the sale is recorded.

  <ParamField body="Payment Reference Label" type="string">
    The label printed on the receipt for the reference number the customer should include in their transfer (e.g., "Invoice No." or "Order Ref.").
  </ParamField>

  <ParamField body="Bank Account Details on Receipt" type="boolean">
    When enabled, your IBAN/account number and bank name are printed on the receipt to make it easy for the customer to complete the transfer.
  </ParamField>

  <Warning>
    Bank transfer sales are recorded as paid in FacilPOS immediately. Reconcile these transactions in FacilERP Accounting once the transfer is confirmed in your bank account.
  </Warning>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Custom Payment Type">
  Add your own payment label for any tender not covered by the built-in types — gift cards from a third-party provider, meal vouchers, cryptocurrency, and so on.

  <Steps>
    <Step title="Add a Custom Type">
      Click **Add Payment Method** and select **Custom**.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Name and Icon">
      Enter a **Display Name** (shown to cashiers at checkout) and optionally choose an icon from the library.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Accounting Account">
      Link the payment type to an **Accounting Account** in FacilERP so that transactions post to the correct ledger account automatically.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Save and Enable">
      Click **Save**, then toggle the method **On**. It will appear as an option during checkout immediately.
    </Step>
  </Steps>
</Accordion>

## Enabling and Disabling Payment Methods

Each payment method has an **Enabled** toggle at the top of its settings panel. Disabling a method hides it from the cashier's payment screen — it will not appear as an option during checkout until you re-enable it.

<Tip>
  Enable only the payment methods you actively use. A focused payment screen reduces cashier hesitation and speeds up checkout, especially during busy periods.
</Tip>

## Card Terminal Integration

### Supported Terminals

FacilPOS connects to payment terminals over your local network (IP pairing) or via USB/serial connection. Check the **Compatible Hardware** list in **Help → Hardware Setup** for models that have been tested and certified.

### Pairing a Terminal

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect the Terminal to Your Network">
    Plug the terminal into your network switch or connect it to the same Wi-Fi network as your POS workstation. Note the terminal's IP address from its network settings screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Terminal Settings">
    In **Configuration → Payment Methods**, expand **Credit / Debit Card** and click **Manage Terminals → Add Terminal**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Connection Details">
    Select the connection type (**IP** or **Serial/USB**). For IP pairing, enter the terminal's IP address and port. For serial, select the correct COM port from the dropdown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the Terminal">
    Give the terminal a descriptive name (e.g., "Register 1 – Countertop"). This name appears in transaction logs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and Test">
    Click **Save Terminal**. The terminal entry appears in the list with a **Test Connection** button — use it to confirm the link before going live.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Testing the Terminal Connection

Click **Test Connection** next to any paired terminal to send a test ping. A green **Connected** badge confirms the terminal is reachable. If the test fails:

1. Confirm the terminal is powered on and connected to the network.
2. Verify the IP address or port has not changed (terminals with DHCP can receive a new IP after a reboot — consider assigning a static IP).
3. Check that no firewall rules are blocking the connection on the port used.

## Cash Rounding Rules

If your local currency has discontinued small denominations, set a rounding rule so that cash totals are rounded to the nearest practical increment. The rounding difference is recorded as a minor gain or loss in your accounting reports and does not affect card or other non-cash payments.

| Rounding Setting | Example Total | Rounded Total | Difference |
|---|---|---|---|
| No rounding | $10.03 | $10.03 | $0.00 |
| Round to 0.05 | $10.03 | $10.05 | +$0.02 |
| Round to 0.10 | $10.03 | $10.00 | −$0.03 |
| Round to 1.00 | $10.60 | $11.00 | +$0.40 |
