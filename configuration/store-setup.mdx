Store setup is where you define the foundational information that FacilPOS uses across every sale, receipt, and report. Getting these settings right before you go live ensures that your receipts look professional, your taxes calculate correctly, and your team works in the right time zone and currency from day one.

## Navigating to Store Setup

To open the store configuration panel, go to **FacilPOS → Configuration → Store Setup**. You must have a Manager or Admin role to view and edit these settings.

---

## General Settings

The general settings panel controls the basic identity and locale of your store.

<ParamField body="Store Name" type="string" required>
  The trading name of your business. This appears at the top of every printed and emailed receipt.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Store Address" type="string">
  Your full street address, including city, state/province, and postal code. Printed on receipts and used on tax documents.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Default Currency" type="select" required>
  The currency used for all prices, totals, and reports. Set this before you create any products — changing it later does not convert existing prices.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Time Zone" type="select" required>
  The local time zone for your store. All sales timestamps, session open/close times, and scheduled reports use this setting.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Date Format" type="select">
  Choose between regional date formats (e.g., DD/MM/YYYY or MM/DD/YYYY). Applied to receipts and all on-screen date displays.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Number Format" type="select">
  Controls the thousands separator and decimal symbol (e.g., 1,000.00 vs. 1.000,00).
</ParamField>

## Tax Configuration

FacilPOS supports flat tax rates, tax-inclusive pricing, and multiple rates for different product categories.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Default Tax Rate">
    Set a single default tax rate that applies to any product without a specific tax assignment.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open Tax Settings">
        Within **Store Setup**, scroll to the **Tax Configuration** section.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Enter the Default Rate">
        Type the percentage in the **Default Tax Rate** field (e.g., `20` for 20%).
      </Step>
      <Step title="Choose Pricing Mode">
        Select either **Tax-exclusive** (tax is added on top of the listed price) or **Tax-inclusive** (tax is already embedded in the listed price). This setting affects how prices are displayed on the POS screen and on receipts.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Save">
        Click **Save Settings**. The new rate applies to the next sale.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Multiple Tax Rates">
    If your jurisdiction requires different rates for different product types (e.g., standard goods vs. food vs. services), you can define multiple rates and assign them at the product level.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Add a Tax Rate">
        Click **Add Tax Rate**, enter a name (e.g., "Reduced Rate – Food") and the percentage.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Assign to Products">
        Navigate to **Inventory → Products**, open a product, and select the appropriate tax rate from the **Tax Rate** dropdown.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Verify on Receipt">
        Process a test sale and confirm the receipt shows the correct tax breakdown for each rate.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Note>
      Products with no explicit tax assignment automatically use the **Default Tax Rate** you configured above.
    </Note>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Receipt Customization

Customize what appears on printed and digital receipts to reinforce your brand and communicate important information to customers.

<Accordion title="Header and Footer Text">
  - **Header Text** — Shown just below your store name. Use it for a tagline or welcome message, e.g., *"Thank you for shopping with us!"*
  - **Footer Text** — Shown at the bottom of the receipt. Ideal for your website URL, return policy summary, or social media handles.

  Both fields support plain text up to 255 characters. Line breaks are preserved.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Receipt Logo">
  Upload your business logo to have it printed at the top of every receipt.

  <Steps>
    <Step title="Upload the Logo">
      In the **Receipt Logo** section, click **Upload Image** and select a PNG or JPEG file. Recommended size: **300 × 100 px** at 300 dpi for crisp thermal printing.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Preview">
      Use the **Receipt Preview** panel on the right to see how the logo renders on a standard receipt width.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Save">
      Click **Save Settings** to apply the logo to all future receipts.
    </Step>
  </Steps>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Show / Hide Receipt Fields">
  Toggle each field on or off depending on your business needs:

  | Field | Default | Notes |
  |---|---|---|
  | Tax breakdown per line | On | Required in most jurisdictions |
  | Cashier name | On | Turn off for privacy |
  | Register / terminal ID | On | Useful for multi-register stores |
  | Loyalty points earned | On | Requires loyalty module active |
  | Customer name | On | Only shown when a customer is linked to the sale |
  | Barcode / QR code | Off | Enable to allow receipt scanning for returns |
</Accordion>

## Session Settings

Session settings control how cashiers open and close their register sessions each day.

<ParamField body="Require Opening Float" type="boolean">
  When enabled, cashiers must declare the opening cash amount before the session begins. This amount is used to calculate the expected closing balance.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Auto-Close Session at End of Day" type="boolean">
  When enabled, FacilPOS automatically closes any open session at the time you specify. Useful for businesses that forget to run the end-of-day process manually. The cashier receives a notification before the session closes.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Auto-Close Time" type="time">
  The time at which sessions are automatically closed. Only available when **Auto-Close Session at End of Day** is enabled.
</ParamField>

<Warning>
  Auto-closing a session does not print a closing report automatically. Remind your team to run the cash register report before leaving, or enable the **Print on Auto-Close** option.
</Warning>
