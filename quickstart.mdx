---

This guide walks you through everything you need to do to go live with FacilPOS for the first time. By the end you will have configured your store, added a product, opened a cash register session, and completed a real sale with a printed or emailed receipt — the full loop from setup to transaction in one sitting.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to FacilERP and open FacilPOS">
    Sign in to your FacilERP account with your usual credentials. From the main navigation menu, select **FacilPOS**. If you do not see the module listed, ask your administrator to grant you the appropriate role — at minimum you need the **Cashier** role to access the POS interface.

    Once inside FacilPOS, you land on the Dashboard, which shows your store's live sales summary, open register sessions, and quick-action shortcuts.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure your store settings">
    Before processing any sales, make sure your store details, currency, and tax rules are set correctly. Navigate to **FacilPOS → Configuration → Store Setup** and fill in:

    - **Store Name** and address — these appear on every receipt.
    - **Default Currency** — all prices and totals display in this currency.
    - **Tax Configuration** — choose whether prices are entered tax-inclusive or tax-exclusive, and assign the correct tax rates.
    - **Receipt Settings** — select print, email, or both, and upload your logo if desired.

    Save your changes before continuing.

    <Tip>
      Store Setup has many additional options — receipt footers, rounding rules, and multi-location settings. See the full [Store Setup guide](/configuration/store-setup) when you are ready to fine-tune your configuration.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add at least one product to the catalog">
    FacilPOS sells products defined in your FacilERP inventory catalog. Navigate to **FacilPOS → Inventory → Products** and create your first product:

    1. Click **New Product**.
    2. Enter a **Product Name** and **SKU** (or let FacilERP generate one).
    3. Set the **Sales Price** and assign a **Category**.
    4. Enter an **Opening Stock** quantity so inventory tracking starts correctly.
    5. Click **Save**.

    The product is now searchable and scannable at the POS counter.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open a cash register session">
    Every sales day starts with opening a register session. This records your opening float — the cash you place in the drawer before trading begins — so that your end-of-day cash count is accurate.

    Navigate to **FacilPOS → Dashboard** and click **Open Session**. Enter the **Opening Float** amount (the cash in the drawer right now) and click **Confirm**. Your session is now active and you can begin processing sales.

    <Note>
      You can only process sales while a register session is open. If the session was closed by a previous user, you must open a new one before the POS accepts transactions.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Process your first sale and issue a receipt">
    With a session open, navigate to **FacilPOS → New Sale**. This opens the sale screen:

    1. **Add products** — search by name or SKU, or scan a barcode. Each item appears in the order line with its price and quantity.
    2. **Adjust quantity or apply a discount** — tap any line to edit the quantity or add a line-level discount if needed.
    3. **Select a payment method** — click **Pay**, choose **Cash**, **Card**, or another configured method, and enter the amount tendered.
    4. **Complete the sale** — click **Confirm Payment**. FacilPOS records the transaction, deducts the sold quantity from stock, and prompts you to issue a receipt.
    5. **Issue the receipt** — choose **Print**, **Email**, or **Skip** based on your customer's preference.

    Congratulations — your first FacilPOS sale is complete!
  </Step>
</Steps>
