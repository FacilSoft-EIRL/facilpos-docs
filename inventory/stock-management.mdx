Accurate stock data means you never sell what you do not have and never miss a reorder until it is too late. FacilPOS tracks inventory in real time — every sale reduces on-hand quantities, every return restores them, and every purchase order received in FacilERP flows through automatically. This page explains how to read your stock levels, respond to alerts, make manual corrections, and run a full physical count.

## View Current Stock Levels

Go to **FacilPOS → Inventory → Products**. The product list includes a **Stock** column showing the current on-hand quantity for every product with stock tracking enabled. Use the column header to sort by stock level — ascending order surfaces your lowest-stock items first.

To see the full history of stock movements for a single product:

1. Click the product name to open its detail view.
2. Select the **Stock History** tab.

Each row in the history shows the date, movement type (sale, return, adjustment, or purchase receipt), quantity changed, and the user who triggered it.

## Set Low-Stock Alerts

Protect yourself from unexpected stockouts by setting a minimum quantity threshold for each product.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the product">
    Navigate to **Inventory → Products** and click the product you want to configure.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the minimum stock threshold">
    In the **Inventory** section of the product form, enter a value in the **Minimum Stock Quantity** field. When on-hand quantity falls to or below this number, the product is flagged in the catalog and appears in the **Low Stock** report.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Changes**. The threshold takes effect immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Low-stock alerts appear on the FacilPOS dashboard as a summary widget and in **Reports → Inventory Reports**. You can also configure email notifications in **Configuration → Store Setup → Notifications**.
</Info>

## How Stock Moves Automatically

FacilPOS updates stock quantities without any manual action in the following situations:

| Event | Effect on Stock |
|---|---|
| Sale completed | Decreases by the quantity sold |
| Sale voided | Restores the quantity sold |
| Return processed | Increases by the quantity returned |
| Purchase order received (FacilERP) | Increases by the quantity received |

<Note>
  Stock only moves when a transaction is **completed** or **confirmed**. Saving a sale as a draft or parking a ticket does not reduce stock.
</Note>

## Manual Stock Adjustment

Use a manual adjustment to correct discrepancies found during a physical count, record damaged or stolen goods, or fix a data entry error.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the adjustment form">
    Go to **Inventory → Products**, find the product, and click **Adjust Stock** (the sliders icon on the right of the row).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the adjustment details">

    <ParamField body="Adjustment Type" type="select" required>
      Choose the reason for the adjustment:
      - **Count Correction** — on-hand quantity differs from a physical count
      - **Damaged Goods** — items are no longer sellable
      - **Theft / Loss** — items are missing
      - **Other** — free-text reason required
    </ParamField>

    <ParamField body="Quantity" type="number" required>
      Enter a positive number to increase stock or a negative number to decrease it.
    </ParamField>

    <ParamField body="Notes" type="string">
      Add context for the adjustment. This note is stored in the stock history and visible to managers in reports.
    </ParamField>

  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the adjustment">
    Click **Apply Adjustment**. FacilPOS prompts for supervisor authorization — a user with the **Inventory Manager** or **Administrator** role must enter their PIN or password to proceed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Manual adjustments are logged permanently and cannot be undone. If you enter an incorrect value, create a second adjustment in the opposite direction to correct it.
</Warning>

## Run a Physical Stock Count

A full stock count lets you reconcile your system quantities against what is physically on your shelves.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new stock count">
    Go to **Inventory → Stock Count** and click **New Count**. FacilPOS freezes a snapshot of current on-hand quantities for comparison.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Count and enter quantities">
    Work through your products aisle by aisle. For each item, enter the physically counted quantity in the **Counted** column. You can complete the count across multiple sessions — progress is saved automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review variances">
    When you have entered all quantities, click **Review**. FacilPOS highlights every product where the counted quantity differs from the system quantity, showing the variance and its estimated cost impact.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply the count">
    Review the variance list, add notes to any unexpected discrepancies, then click **Apply Count**. FacilPOS creates automatic adjustments (type: **Count Correction**) for every variance and updates on-hand quantities. Supervisor authorization is required.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Schedule physical stock counts during off-hours or after closing to avoid discrepancies caused by sales made during the count process.
</Tip>

## ERP Integration: Purchase Orders

When a purchase order is received in FacilERP, the incoming quantities are pushed to FacilPOS automatically — no manual entry needed. The movement appears in the product's Stock History as **Purchase Receipt** with a reference to the FacilERP purchase order number.

To review which purchase orders have updated your POS stock, go to **Reports → Inventory Reports** and filter by movement type **Purchase Receipt**.

<Warning>
  If you disable stock tracking on a product (in the product's edit form), FacilPOS stops recording quantity movements for that item and no low-stock alerts will fire — even if the product runs out. Only disable tracking for service items or products you intentionally do not count.
</Warning>
