Categories bring structure to your product catalog and directly shape how staff interact with the POS terminal. A well-organized category tree lets cashiers tap into the right product group in seconds, reduces search time during busy periods, and keeps your POS grid tidy whether you have 50 products or 5,000.

## Navigate to Categories

Go to **FacilPOS → Inventory → Categories**. The page shows all existing categories in a tree view, with top-level categories expanded to reveal any subcategories beneath them.

## Create a Category

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the New Category form">
    Click **+ New Category** in the top-right corner of the Categories page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the category">

    <ParamField body="Name" type="string" required>
      The label displayed on the POS product grid button and in the catalog filter. Use a short, recognizable name (e.g., **Beverages**, **Electronics**, **Accessories**).
    </ParamField>

    <ParamField body="Color" type="color picker">
      Choose a background color for the category's button on the POS grid. Using distinct colors for top-level categories helps cashiers navigate the grid at a glance.
    </ParamField>

    <ParamField body="Icon" type="select">
      Pick an icon from the built-in library to display alongside the category name on the POS grid button. Icons reinforce visual recognition, especially in high-volume environments.
    </ParamField>

    <ParamField body="Parent Category" type="select">
      Leave blank to create a top-level category. To create a subcategory, select an existing category as the parent. FacilPOS supports one level of nesting — subcategories cannot themselves have child categories.
    </ParamField>

  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the category">
    Click **Save Category**. The new category appears immediately in the tree view and becomes available when assigning products and configuring the POS grid.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Edit a Category

1. Click the category name or the **Edit** (pencil) icon in the tree view.
2. Update the name, color, icon, or parent category as needed.
3. Click **Save Changes**.

Renaming a category updates it everywhere it appears — in the catalog, on the POS grid, and in reports — without affecting the products assigned to it.

## Delete a Category

1. Click the **Delete** (trash) icon next to the category.
2. Confirm the action in the dialog that appears.

<Warning>
  You cannot delete a category that still has products assigned to it. Reassign or deactivate those products first, or move them to another category, before deleting.
</Warning>

## How Categories Appear in the POS Grid

On the POS terminal, the product grid displays a row of category buttons at the top. Tapping a top-level category filters the product tiles below to show only items in that group. If subcategories exist, a second row of sub-filter buttons appears beneath the top-level selection.

<Info>
  You can reorder category buttons on the POS grid by dragging them in **Configuration → Store Setup → Grid Layout**. Arrange your most-used categories toward the left for faster access.
</Info>

## Nested Categories (Subcategories)

Subcategories let you break broad groups into more specific sections without cluttering the top-level grid. For example:

| Top-Level Category | Subcategories |
|--------------------|---------------|
| Beverages | Hot Drinks, Cold Drinks, Alcohol |
| Food | Snacks, Meals, Desserts |
| Electronics | Cables, Batteries, Accessories |

At the POS terminal, selecting **Beverages** reveals buttons for **Hot Drinks**, **Cold Drinks**, and **Alcohol** — staff tap once more to reach the specific product tiles.

<Tip>
  Keep your top-level categories broad and limit the number to what fits comfortably on the POS screen without scrolling. Use subcategories for the specifics. Aim for no more than 8–10 top-level categories.
</Tip>
