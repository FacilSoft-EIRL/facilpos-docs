Your product catalog is the foundation of FacilPOS — every item you sell, its price, tax rate, and stock level lives here. Keeping your catalog accurate and up to date ensures smooth checkouts, reliable inventory counts, and consistent reporting across FacilERP.

## Navigate to the Product Catalog

To open the product catalog, go to **FacilPOS → Inventory → Products**. The list view displays all active products with their SKU, category, sale price, and current stock quantity. Use the search bar or category filter at the top to quickly locate a specific item.

## Create a New Product

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the New Product form">
    From the Products list, click **+ New Product** in the top-right corner. The product creation form opens in a side panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the product details">
    Complete the following fields:

    <ParamField body="Product Name" type="string" required>
      The display name shown at the POS terminal and on receipts. Keep it concise and recognizable to your staff.
    </ParamField>

    <ParamField body="SKU / Barcode" type="string">
      Enter a unique SKU manually or scan a barcode with a connected barcode scanner. FacilPOS uses this value for fast product lookup during a sale.
    </ParamField>

    <ParamField body="Category" type="select">
      Assign the product to an existing category to organize the POS grid. See [Categories](/inventory/categories) for how to create and manage categories.
    </ParamField>

    <ParamField body="Sale Price" type="number" required>
      The price charged to the customer. Taxes are calculated on top of this value based on the tax rate you set below.
    </ParamField>

    <ParamField body="Cost Price" type="number">
      The amount your business pays to acquire or produce the item. Used in margin and profitability reports — not shown to customers.
    </ParamField>

    <ParamField body="Tax Rate" type="select">
      Choose the applicable tax rate from the preconfigured options. To add new tax rates, go to **Configuration → Store Setup**.
    </ParamField>

    <ParamField body="Stock Tracking" type="toggle">
      Enable this toggle to have FacilPOS track quantity on hand for this product. Disable it for service items or products you do not physically count (e.g., gift wrapping).
    </ParamField>

    <ParamField body="Initial Stock Quantity" type="number">
      Visible only when stock tracking is enabled. Enter the quantity currently on hand. You can adjust this later through a manual stock adjustment.
    </ParamField>

    <ParamField body="Description / Notes" type="string">
      Optional internal notes or a customer-facing product description. This field appears on detailed receipts if your receipt template is configured to show it.
    </ParamField>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the product">
    Click **Save Product**. The item immediately appears in your product catalog and becomes available for selection at the POS terminal.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Edit an Existing Product

1. Locate the product in the catalog list or use the search bar.
2. Click the product name or the **Edit** (pencil) icon on the right.
3. Update any fields as needed, then click **Save Changes**.

Price changes take effect immediately for new transactions. Open tickets that already contain the product are not retroactively updated.

## Deactivate a Product

Deactivating a product removes it from the POS terminal without permanently deleting its sales history.

1. Open the product's edit form.
2. Toggle the **Active** switch to off.
3. Click **Save Changes**.

To restore a deactivated product, filter the catalog list by **Inactive**, open the product, and toggle **Active** back on.

<Note>
  Deleting a product is permanent and removes it from historical reports. Deactivation is recommended in almost all cases.
</Note>

## Bulk Import via Spreadsheet

If you need to add a large number of products at once, use the bulk import tool:

1. Go to **Inventory → Products** and click **Import**.
2. Download the provided CSV template and fill in one product per row.
3. Upload the completed file and review the validation summary — any rows with errors are highlighted for correction.
4. Click **Confirm Import** to add all valid rows to your catalog.

<Tip>
  Include the `barcode` column in your import file to enable instant barcode scanning for each product from day one.
</Tip>

## Sync with FacilERP

<Tip>
  Products sync automatically with the FacilERP purchasing module. When a purchase order is received in FacilERP, stock quantities in FacilPOS update in real time — no manual entry required. See [Stock Management](/inventory/stock-management) for details on how stock levels are maintained.
</Tip>
