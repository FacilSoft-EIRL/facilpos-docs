---
title: 'Setting Up Price Lists for FacilPOS'
sidebarTitle: 'Price Lists'
description: 'Create custom price lists for VIP customers, wholesale accounts, and seasonal promotions, and let FacilPOS apply them automatically at checkout.'
---

Price lists give you flexible control over what different customers pay. Rather than manually applying discounts at the register, you define a price list once — specifying custom prices or percentage discounts per product — and assign it to a customer group. When a cashier selects a customer at checkout, FacilPOS automatically applies that customer's price list, eliminating guesswork and pricing errors.

## Navigate to Price Lists

Go to **FacilPOS → Configuration → Price Lists**. The page shows all price lists with their name, currency, assigned customer group, and active date range.

## Create a Price List

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the New Price List form">
    Click **+ New Price List** in the top-right corner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the price list properties">

    <ParamField body="Name" type="string" required>
      A clear, internal label that describes the list's purpose — for example, **Wholesale**, **VIP Members**, or **Summer Promotion 2025**. This name appears in customer group settings and checkout logs.
    </ParamField>

    <ParamField body="Currency" type="select" required>
      Select the currency this list applies to. If your store operates in a single currency, this defaults to your base currency. Multi-currency support requires the **International** add-on in FacilERP.
    </ParamField>

    <ParamField body="Valid From" type="date">
      The date on which this price list becomes active. Leave blank to make it active immediately upon saving.
    </ParamField>

    <ParamField body="Valid To" type="date">
      The date on which this price list expires. After this date, FacilPOS falls back to the default price list for customers assigned to this list. Leave blank for no expiry.
    </ParamField>

  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the price list">
    Click **Save Price List**. The list is created in draft state — no products have custom prices yet. Continue to the next section to add products.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add Products to a Price List

After saving the price list, you land on its detail page. The **Products** tab lists every item in your catalog. For each product you want to override, choose a pricing method:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Fixed Price">
    Enter a specific sale price in the **Custom Price** field. FacilPOS uses this exact amount for customers on this list, regardless of the product's default sale price.

    Use fixed prices when you have negotiated a set unit price with a wholesale account or want a stable promotional price.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Percentage Discount">
    Enter a discount percentage in the **Discount %** field (e.g., `15` for 15% off). FacilPOS calculates the final price at checkout by applying the discount to the product's current default sale price.

    Use percentage discounts when you want the price list to stay proportional if your base prices change — useful for ongoing loyalty discounts.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Products with no custom price or discount set in a price list are sold at their default price, even when the list is active.

<Tip>
  Use the **Import** button on the Products tab to upload a CSV with custom prices for many products at once. Download the template first to see the required column format.
</Tip>

## Assign a Price List to a Customer Group

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Customer Groups">
    Navigate to **Customers → Customer Management → Groups** (or open the price list's **Assignments** tab and click **Assign to Group**).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select or create a group">
    Choose an existing customer group — such as **Wholesale Accounts** or **VIP Members** — or create a new one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the price list">
    In the group settings, select your new price list from the **Default Price List** dropdown and save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Every customer who belongs to that group will now have this price list applied automatically at checkout.

## How FacilPOS Applies Price Lists at Checkout

When a cashier adds products to a sale and then selects a customer, FacilPOS checks whether that customer's group has an active price list. If it does, item prices in the current sale update instantly to reflect the list's custom prices or discounts. The cashier does not need to take any additional action.

<Info>
  Price list application happens at the time the customer is selected. If you add more products to the sale after selecting the customer, those items are also priced using the active list.
</Info>

The receipt and transaction record both show the final price paid and, where a discount was applied, the original price and discount amount for transparency.

## Default Price List vs. Custom Price Lists

<Accordion title="What is the default price list?">
  The default price list reflects each product's standard sale price as configured in the product catalog. Every customer who is not assigned to a group with a custom price list pays the default price. You cannot delete the default price list, but you can edit individual product prices directly in **Inventory → Products**.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens when a price list expires?">
  Once the **Valid To** date passes, FacilPOS automatically stops applying the price list and falls back to the default price list for all customers assigned to that group. You do not need to manually deactivate it. The expired list remains in the system for reference and can be cloned to create a new seasonal promotion.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can a customer have more than one price list?">
  Each customer group can have one active price list at a time. If you need to stack promotions (e.g., a wholesale discount plus a seasonal offer), create a combined price list that incorporates both discounts rather than assigning multiple lists.
</Accordion>

<Tip>
  For time-limited promotions such as a holiday sale or end-of-season clearance, set a **Valid From** and **Valid To** date range when creating the price list. FacilPOS activates and deactivates the list automatically — no manual intervention needed on the start or end date.
</Tip>
