FacilPOS is the point-of-sale module built into FacilERP, designed for store managers, cashiers, and business owners who need a fast, reliable way to process sales and keep their operations in sync. Because FacilPOS runs inside FacilERP rather than as a standalone application, every transaction you record — from a simple cash sale to a multi-tender return — automatically updates your inventory levels, customer records, and financial ledgers without any manual data entry.

## Who FacilPOS Is For

FacilPOS serves three primary roles across a retail or hospitality business:

- **Cashiers** — process sales, accept payments, issue receipts, and handle returns at the point of sale.
- **Store Managers** — open and close cash register sessions, monitor daily sales, manage products and price lists, and run shift reports.
- **Business Owners & Administrators** — configure store settings, define user permissions, integrate payment terminals, and analyse performance through cross-store reports.

## Key Capabilities

FacilPOS covers every stage of the customer transaction lifecycle:

- **Sales Processing** — scan or search products, apply discounts, split payments across multiple methods, and complete a sale in seconds.
- **Inventory Management** — track stock levels in real time, receive purchase orders, and get low-stock alerts without leaving the POS interface.
- **Customer Management** — look up customer accounts at checkout, apply loyalty rewards, and keep a full purchase history per customer.
- **Payments** — accept cash, card, mobile wallets, and store credit; configure each payment method to match your hardware and business rules.
- **Reporting** — generate end-of-day cash register reports, sales summaries, and inventory movement reports directly from the POS.
- **ERP Integration** — sales data flows automatically into FacilERP's accounting, purchasing, and CRM modules so your back-office always reflects what happens on the shop floor.

## How FacilPOS Fits Inside FacilERP

FacilPOS is a module within FacilERP, not a separate product. You access it through the FacilERP navigation menu, and it shares the same database, user directory, and configuration framework as every other FacilERP module. This means a product you create in the Inventory module is immediately available at the POS, a customer registered in the CRM can be found at checkout, and every sale you post flows straight into your accounts without an import step.

<Note>
  FacilPOS requires an active FacilERP installation. You must have a valid FacilERP license and at least one configured store before you can use FacilPOS features. If you have not yet set up FacilERP, contact your system administrator or implementation partner before proceeding.
</Note>
